## Purpose

Provide durable, evidence-backed launch, community, comparison, and search materials that let Panerelay promote one coherent product position without inventing claims or repeating generic advertising across communities.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Canonical marketing narrative

The repository SHALL provide an English and Simplified Chinese marketing source of truth that leads with reuse of the user's everyday Chrome or Edge session, the user's choice between current-tab and all-supported-tabs authorization, and active control that remains separately visible and releasable. It SHALL distinguish product behavior, verified evidence, intended use cases, and explicit non-goals.

#### Scenario: Maintainer prepares a new promotion

- **GIVEN** a maintainer needs to introduce Panerelay in a new channel
- **WHEN** the maintainer consults the marketing source of truth
- **THEN** the resulting introduction can state the core value, authorization model, supported automation integrations, and browser-ownership limits without reconstructing the positioning from source code

### Requirement: Community-specific launch assets

The launch kit SHALL provide ready-to-edit English and Simplified Chinese variants for long-form launch posts, compact social posts, technical communities, product communities, and contextual replies. Each variant SHALL preserve the canonical facts while adapting its hook, level of detail, call to action, disclosure, and formatting to the destination rather than repeating one identical advertisement.

#### Scenario: Operator selects a community draft

- **GIVEN** an operator has identified a relevant public discussion or launch venue
- **WHEN** the operator selects the corresponding launch-kit variant
- **THEN** the draft fits that venue's expected style, discloses the project relationship, contains a useful standalone point, and links to the most relevant Panerelay resource

### Requirement: Evidence-backed comparison claims

The launch kit SHALL maintain a dated claim register for Panerelay, raw CDP attachment, managed or isolated browsers, Browser Use, and the Playwright Chrome Extension. Public comparison wording MUST link to primary upstream documentation or checked-in Panerelay compatibility evidence, distinguish documented facts from inference, and avoid implying that one approach is universally superior.

#### Scenario: Upstream behavior changes

- **GIVEN** an operator is about to publish a comparison claim
- **WHEN** its supporting source is stale, unavailable, or no longer matches the proposed wording
- **THEN** the operator updates or removes the claim instead of publishing it as current fact

### Requirement: Responsible community operation

The operating playbook SHALL require contextual participation, truthful project affiliation, deduplication, platform-rule compliance, and a preference for answering the discussion's actual question before mentioning Panerelay. It MUST prohibit fabricated testimonials, impersonation, mass-posted replies, bought engagement, disparagement, secret disclosure, and attempts to bypass login, moderation, rate limits, or verification challenges.

#### Scenario: No high-quality opportunity exists

- **GIVEN** an hourly promotion review finds no discussion where Panerelay can add specific value
- **WHEN** the operator decides whether to engage
- **THEN** the operator records no public action rather than forcing a post to satisfy the review interval

### Requirement: Search content map

The launch kit SHALL map English and Simplified Chinese search intents to existing or proposed pages, primary phrases, supporting phrases, user questions, internal links, and evidence needs. It SHALL prioritize queries that Panerelay can answer honestly and SHALL NOT create doorway pages or near-duplicate keyword variants.

#### Scenario: SEO opportunity is selected

- **GIVEN** a relevant search phrase has been identified
- **WHEN** the team chooses to publish content for it
- **THEN** the content map identifies one canonical page, a distinct user intent, supporting evidence, and a useful next action without keyword stuffing
